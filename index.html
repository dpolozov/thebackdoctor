Our Services

A Modern Approach

Every body is different. Our treatments are tailored to your specific biomechanics and goals.

01
Chiropractic Adjustments
Precision spinal and joint manipulation to improve mobility and nervous system function.
02
Manual Soft Tissue Therapy
Targeted work on muscles and fascia to release tension and improve recovery.
03
Rehabilitative Exercise
Custom movement protocols to build strength and prevent future injury.
04
Myofascial Release
Targeted manual therapy to release tension in the connective tissue (fascia), improving blood flow and accelerating muscle recovery after intense activity.
05
Kinesio Taping
Strategic taping to support unstable joints and tired muscles without limiting range of motion—essential for athletes returning to play.
06
Postural & Ergonomic Correction
Comprehensive assessments of your daily movement patterns to identify habits that cause recurring pain, with custom plans to fix them.
07
Neuromuscular Re-education
Specialized exercises designed to "reset" the communication between your brain and muscles, restoring proper movement patterns and balance.
